Grilled Chicken Fajitas

Outdoor Grill ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 15m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5–2 lbs chicken breast tenders
  • 3 bell peppers (red, yellow, orange), sliced
  • 1 large yellow onion, sliced
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed & drained
  • 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 lime (juiced)
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning (chili powder, cumin, garlic, paprika, salt)
  • Flour / corn tortillas (for kids/dad)
  • Toppings: fresh salsa, 1 avocado, sour cream
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Preheat outdoor grill to medium-high (400°F). Lightly oil grill grates or use a grill basket for peppers and onions.
  2. Toss chicken tenders and sliced peppers/onions in olive oil, lime juice, and fajita seasoning.
  3. Grill chicken for 4–5 minutes per side until internal temp reaches 165°F. Grill peppers and onions in grill basket for 8–10 minutes until tender with light char. Warm black beans in a small saucepan.
  4. Remove chicken and rest for 3 minutes, then slice into thin strips. Warm tortillas on the grill for 30 seconds.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz grilled chicken breast + 1/2 cup warm black beans + 1 cup grilled peppers/onions + 1/4 sliced avocado + 2 tbsp fresh salsa.
🎯 38g Protein • 15g Fat • 44g Carbs • 460 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Top generously with hot salsa, fresh sliced jalapeños, and a squeeze of fresh lime.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Warm flour tortillas with sliced mild chicken and mild shredded Mexican cheese blend (peppers on side).

Air-Fryer Lemon Herb Chicken & Potato Wedges

Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 18m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs chicken breast tenderloins
  • 3 large Russet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 lb fresh green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon (zested and juiced)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano & 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt & black pepper
  • Deli Tzatziki dip & crumbled feta
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Preheat Air Fryer to 400°F. Toss potato wedges with 1 tbsp oil, salt, and garlic powder. Air-fry for 15–18 mins, shaking halfway, until crisp and golden.
  2. Meanwhile, marinate chicken tenders in 1 tbsp olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Steam or sauté green beans in a pan with 1 tbsp water and light olive oil for 5 mins until bright green and tender-crisp.
  4. Air-fry chicken at 380°F for 10–12 minutes, turning at 6 minutes, until juicy and internal temp is 165°F.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz lemon herb chicken breast + 140g crispy roasted potato wedges + 1 cup steamed green beans + 2 tbsp light tzatziki.
🎯 38g Protein • 13g Fat • 45g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Serve chicken topped with generous cool tzatziki, crumbled feta, and spicy chili oil.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Mild tender slices + crispy potato wedges + green beans with ketchup or mild honey mustard.

Instant Pot Turkey Meatballs in Marinara

Instant Pot ⏱️ Prep: 12m 🔥 Cook: 7m (High) 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5–2 lbs ground turkey (93/7)
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs + 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 jar (24 oz) quality marinara sauce + 1/2 cup broth
  • 12 oz penne or rotini pasta (for kids/dad)
  • 2 oz Banza chickpea pasta + 2 medium zucchinis (zoodles) for mom
  • 1 crown broccoli (steamed for side)
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. In a bowl, gently combine ground turkey, panko, egg, Parmesan, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and salt. Roll into 16–18 round meatballs.
  2. Pour marinara sauce and 1/2 cup broth into Instant Pot. Gently submerge raw meatballs in the sauce.
  3. Seal lid, cook on High Pressure for 7 minutes. Boil family pasta and Mom's Banza pasta; steam broccoli. Spiralize zucchini into zoodles.
  4. Quick Release pressure. Sauté zoodles for 2 mins in a pan with 1 tsp olive oil. Toss zoodles with cooked Banza pasta, spoon 4 meatballs and marinara over top.
🥗 Mom's Portion
4 lean turkey meatballs + 1.5 oz Banza chickpea pasta combined with 1 cup sautéed zoodles + 1/2 cup marinara + side green salad.
🎯 36g Protein • 14g Fat • 46g Carbs • 460 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Extra red pepper flakes, fresh shaved parmesan, and fresh cracked black pepper over pasta.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Penne pasta with marinara lightly tossed (or on side), 2 meatballs, and steamed broccoli trees with butter.

Grilled Balsamic Herb Chicken Breasts with Sweet Potato Wedges & Zucchini

Outdoor Grill ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 15m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5–2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts or tenderloins
  • 3 medium sweet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 2 medium zucchinis, cut lengthwise into spears
  • 2 tbsp olive oil + 2 tbsp balsamic glaze/vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt & pepper
  • Coarse whole-grain Dijon mustard (for dipping)
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Toss sweet potato wedges in 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and rosemary. Roast in air fryer or oven at 400°F for 20 mins until tender and caramelized.
  2. Preheat grill to medium-high (400°F). Rub chicken breasts and zucchini spears with olive oil, balsamic glaze, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Grill chicken breasts for 5–6 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Grill zucchini spears for 3–4 minutes per side until marked and tender.
  4. Let chicken rest 3 minutes before slicing for the family.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz balsamic herb grilled chicken breast + 130g roasted sweet potato wedges + 1.5 cups grilled zucchini spears.
🎯 37g Protein • 12g Fat • 44g Carbs • 440 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Spicy coarse whole-grain mustard dip on the side with a dash of hot pepper flakes.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Tender grilled chicken slices + sweet potato wedges (naturally sweet) + zucchini coins.

Custom Family Turkey Burgers (with Sweet Potato Fries & DIY Burger Bar)

Outdoor Grill ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 10m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5–2 lbs 93/7 lean ground turkey (Costco)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp Worcestershire
  • 5 Brioche burger buns (plus 1 whole wheat thin bun for mom)
  • 2 sweet potatoes, cut into thin matchstick fries
  • Cheese slices (cheddar for kids, pepper jack for dad)
  • Toppings: Romaine lettuce, sliced tomato, red onion, pickles
  • Condiments: Sriracha mayo, ketchup, mustard
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Toss sweet potato matchsticks with 1 tsp oil, salt, and air-fry at 390°F for 14 mins until crisp.
  2. Form ground turkey into 5 equal patties. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Worcestershire sauce.
  3. Preheat grill to medium-high (400°F). Grill turkey patties for 5 minutes per side until cooked through (165°F internal). Top with cheese during the last minute.
  4. Toast buns on the grill for 30 seconds. Set up DIY burger bar with fresh lettuce, tomatoes, onions, and sauces.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz seasoned turkey patty + open-face whole wheat thin bun + tomato, red onion, lettuce + 100g air-fried sweet potato fries.
🎯 36g Protein • 14g Fat • 44g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Melted pepper jack cheese, pickled jalapeños, red onions, and homemade sriracha mayo on brioche.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Cheesy turkey burger on soft brioche bun with ketchup, pickles, and sweet potato fries.

Air-Fryer Honey-Garlic Salmon (with Kids Tenders Option)

Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 8m 🔥 Cook: 10m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2–3 fresh salmon fillets (approx. 5–6 oz each for parents)
  • Kids meat: 1 lb chicken breast tenders (for air fryer)
  • 2 tbsp honey + 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce (or tamari)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced + 1 tsp grated ginger + 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Cooked brown rice & jasmine rice
  • 1 head fresh broccoli florets, steamed
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Whisk honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, ginger, and lemon juice together in a small bowl.
  2. Brush salmon fillets with olive oil and spoon half the honey-garlic glaze over top.
  3. Preheat Air Fryer to 400°F. Place salmon in basket skin-side down. Air-fry for 7–9 minutes until flaky and edges are caramelized. Brush with remaining glaze.
  4. Kids Chicken Tenders: Bread lightly with panko or cook seasoned tenders in batch 2 for 8–10 mins at 390°F.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz salmon fillet + 2/3 cup cooked brown rice + 1.5 cups steamed broccoli florets + 1 tbsp honey-garlic glaze.
🎯 34g Protein • 16g Fat • 48g Carbs • 470 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Stir sriracha and red pepper flakes directly into his honey-garlic glaze portion before glazing.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Kid Friendly: Crispy air-fryer chicken tenders + fluffy white rice + steamed broccoli with butter.

Instant Pot Teriyaki Chicken with Snap Peas & Peppers

Instant Pot ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 8m (High) 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5–2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts / tenders
  • 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce (or tamari)
  • 3 tbsp honey + 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced + 1 tsp fresh grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water (slurry)
  • 1 bag sugar snap peas & 1 sliced bell pepper
  • Cooked brown rice & jasmine white rice
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Whisk soy sauce, honey, brown sugar, garlic, and ginger directly in the Instant Pot insert. Add chicken breasts and coat in sauce.
  2. Lock lid, cook on High Pressure for 8 minutes. Quick release pressure.
  3. Transfer chicken to a plate and dice or shred into bite-sized pieces. Turn Instant Pot to "Sauté" mode, stir in cornstarch slurry, and simmer 2 mins until glossy and thickened.
  4. Add snap peas and sliced peppers into the hot sauce for 2 minutes to tender-crisp. Return chicken to pot.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz chicken breast + 1/2 cup cooked brown rice + 1.5 cups snap peas & bell peppers + 1 tbsp teriyaki glaze.
🎯 38g Protein • 12g Fat • 48g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Topped with crispy chili crunch oil, sliced green onions, and toasted sesame seeds.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Mild shredded teriyaki chicken over jasmine rice with crisp sliced English cucumbers.

Air-Fryer Crispy Panko Chicken Tenders

Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 12m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs chicken tenderloins
  • 1 cup Panko breadcrumbs + 1 egg (beaten with 1 tbsp water)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt
  • Cooking oil spray (avocado/olive)
  • 1.5 lbs baby petite medley potatoes, halved
  • Large mixed garden salad kit with light lemon-herb dressing
  • Buffalo sauce, Blue cheese dressing, Honey mustard
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Toss halved baby potatoes in 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Air-fry at 400°F for 15 minutes until crispy.
  2. Mix panko with seasonings in a shallow bowl. Dip chicken tenders in beaten egg, then press firmly into panko crumbs.
  3. Preheat Air Fryer to 390°F. Spray basket and tenders with cooking spray. Air-fry for 10–12 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden brown (165°F).
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz crispy panko tenders + 110g roasted baby medley potatoes + large garden salad with light lemon-herb dressing.
🎯 36g Protein • 14g Fat • 44g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Toss 3 tenders in spicy buffalo sauce; serve with creamy blue cheese crumbles.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Ultra-crispy panko tenders with ketchup/honey mustard + crispy roasted potato bites.

20-Minute Savory Chicken & Broccoli Skillet

Stovetop Skillet ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 10m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs chicken breasts, thinly sliced into bite-sized strips
  • 2 large broccoli heads, cut into florets
  • 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce + 1/3 cup chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar or honey + 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced + 1 tsp fresh ginger
  • 1.5 tbsp cornstarch
  • Cooked brown rice & jasmine rice
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Whisk soy sauce, chicken broth, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and cornstarch in a bowl.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add chicken slices in a single layer and sear for 3–4 minutes until golden and cooked through. Remove chicken to a plate.
  3. Add broccoli florets and 3 tbsp water to the pan. Cover and steam for 3 minutes until vibrant green and tender-crisp.
  4. Pour in sauce and bring to a simmer for 1 min until glossy and thick. Return chicken, toss to coat, and serve immediately.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz sliced chicken breast + 1/2 cup cooked brown rice + 1.5 cups steamed broccoli florets + savory ginger-soy sauce.
🎯 38g Protein • 13g Fat • 46g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Top with chili garlic sauce, sriracha, toasted sesame seeds, and extra scallions.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Tender chicken strips over fluffy white rice with mild broccoli florets.

Grilled Cod with Lemon-Caper Butter & Asparagus (Kids Skewers)

Outdoor Grill ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 10m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2–3 thick Cod or white fish fillets (approx. 6 oz each for parents)
  • Kids meat: 1 lb chicken breast chunks on skewers
  • 1 bunch fresh asparagus, woody ends snapped
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted + 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon (juiced and sliced) + 1 tbsp drained capers
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced + fresh parsley
  • Cooked wild rice blend & jasmine rice
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Preheat grill to medium (375°F). Lightly oil foil sheets or grill basket for the delicate fish.
  2. Season cod fillets and asparagus with olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic. Thread chicken chunks onto skewers and season with salt and garlic.
  3. Grill cod on foil for 8–10 minutes until flaky and opaque. Grill asparagus and chicken skewers directly on grates for 8–10 mins, turning often.
  4. Melt butter in a small pan, stir in lemon juice, capers, and minced garlic; spoon over the cooked cod.
🥗 Mom's Portion
6 oz grilled cod fillet + 2/3 cup cooked wild rice blend + 1.5 cups grilled asparagus + 1 tbsp lemon-caper garlic butter.
🎯 36g Protein • 15g Fat • 42g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor

Instant Pot Turkey Burrito Bowls

Instant Pot ⏱️ Prep: 8m 🔥 Cook: 6m (High) 👥 Serves: 5

Sweet & Savory Korean Ground Turkey Skillet

Fish Night Kid Alternate (W1 & W3 Sat)

Air-Fryer Crispy Homemade Chicken Tenders

Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 8m 🔥 Cook: 10m 👥 Serves: 3 Kids
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1 lb chicken breast tenderloins
  • 3/4 cup Panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg, beaten (or 2 tbsp milk)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder & 1/2 tsp salt (mild)
  • Olive oil cooking spray
  • Dipping sauces: Ketchup, mild honey mustard, BBQ sauce
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Preheat Air Fryer to 390°F. Lightly spray basket with cooking oil.
  2. In one shallow bowl, beat the egg. In a second bowl, combine panko, garlic powder, and salt.
  3. Dip each tenderloin into the egg, then dredge in panko, pressing gently so crumbs adhere.
  4. Arrange in a single layer in the air fryer basket. Spray tops lightly with cooking oil.
  5. Air-fry for 10 minutes at 390°F, flipping at 5 minutes, until golden, crispy, and cooked to 165°F.
🧒 Serving Recommendation
Serve with fluffy white/brown rice or roasted potato wedges, sliced cucumbers, and kid's choice of dipping sauce.
