Grilled Chicken Fajitas

Outdoor Grill ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 15m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5–2 lbs chicken breast tenders
  • 3 bell peppers (red, yellow, orange), sliced
  • 1 large yellow onion, sliced
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed & drained
  • 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 lime (juiced)
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning (chili powder, cumin, garlic, paprika, salt)
  • Flour / corn tortillas (for kids/dad)
  • Toppings: fresh salsa, 1 avocado, sour cream
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Preheat outdoor grill to medium-high (400°F). Lightly oil grill grates or use a grill basket for peppers and onions.
  2. Toss chicken tenders and sliced peppers/onions in olive oil, lime juice, and fajita seasoning.
  3. Grill chicken for 4–5 minutes per side until internal temp reaches 165°F. Grill peppers and onions in grill basket for 8–10 minutes until tender with light char. Warm black beans in a small saucepan.
  4. Remove chicken and rest for 3 minutes, then slice into thin strips. Warm tortillas on the grill for 30 seconds.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz grilled chicken breast + 1/2 cup warm black beans + 1 cup grilled peppers/onions + 1/4 sliced avocado + 2 tbsp fresh salsa.
🎯 38g Protein • 15g Fat • 44g Carbs • 460 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Top generously with hot salsa, pickled jalapeño slices, and a fresh lime squeeze.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Warm flour tortillas with sliced mild chicken and mild shredded Mexican cheese blend (peppers on side).

Air-Fryer Lemon Herb Chicken & Potato Wedges

Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 18m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs chicken breast tenderloins
  • 3 large Russet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 lb fresh green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon (zested and juiced)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano & 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt & black pepper
  • Deli Tzatziki dip & crumbled feta
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Preheat Air Fryer to 400°F. Toss potato wedges with 1 tbsp oil, salt, and garlic powder. Air-fry for 15–18 mins, shaking halfway, until crisp and golden.
  2. Meanwhile, marinate chicken tenders in 1 tbsp olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Steam or sauté green beans in a pan with 1 tbsp water and light olive oil for 5 mins until bright green and tender-crisp.
  4. Air-fry chicken at 380°F for 10–12 minutes, turning at 6 minutes, until juicy and internal temp is 165°F.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz lemon herb chicken breast + 140g crispy roasted potato wedges + 1 cup steamed green beans + 2 tbsp light tzatziki.
🎯 38g Protein • 13g Fat • 45g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Serve chicken topped with generous cool tzatziki, crumbled feta, and chili garlic crisp.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Lemon tenders + crispy potato wedges + steamed green beans with ketchup.

🌱 Crispy Sesame Peanut Tofu & Veggie Stir-Fry

Skillet / Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 15m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 blocks (14–16 oz each) organic extra-firm tofu, pressed & cubed
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp olive/sesame oil
  • 1 bag (12 oz) broccoli florets + 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 3 cups cooked brown rice (or jasmine for kids)
  • Quick Peanut Sauce: 3 tbsp creamy peanut butter, 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey/maple syrup, 1 tbsp warm water, 1 clove minced garlic
  • Toppings: crushed salted peanuts, sesame seeds, green onions
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Toss pressed tofu cubes with cornstarch, 1 tbsp oil, and a pinch of salt. Air-fry at 400°F for 12–14 minutes (shaking halfway) or pan-sear in a skillet until golden and crispy.
  2. Whisk peanut butter, soy sauce, honey, warm water, and garlic in a small bowl until smooth.
  3. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, sauté broccoli and sliced bell peppers with 1 tbsp oil for 4–5 minutes until tender-crisp.
  4. Add crispy tofu and peanut sauce to the skillet; toss gently for 1–2 minutes until warm and well coated. Serve over cooked rice.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz air-crisped tofu + 1/2 cup cooked brown rice + 1.5 cups broccoli & peppers tossed in 1.5 tbsp light peanut sauce.
🎯 32g Protein • 16g Fat • 48g Carbs • 460 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Drizzle generously with chili garlic crisp oil, sriracha, and extra crunchy crushed salted peanuts.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Deconstructed: crispy golden tofu nuggets with mild sweet soy dip, jasmine rice, and sweet bell pepper sticks.

🥩 Classic Lean Beef Bolognese with Banza Penne

Stovetop Skillet ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 20m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs 90/10 or 93/7 lean ground beef
  • 1 medium yellow onion & 2 cloves garlic, finely diced
  • 1 jar (24 oz) quality Marinara sauce (e.g. Rao's or Kirkland organic)
  • 1 box (8 oz) Banza chickpea penne (for Mom) + regular penne for kids
  • 1 medium zucchini, spiralized into zoodles (for Mom's pasta blend)
  • 1 bag mixed garden salad greens + light Italian dressing
  • Grated parmesan cheese & crushed red pepper flakes
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown lean ground beef with diced onion and garlic for 6–7 minutes until fully cooked. Drain any excess fat.
  2. Pour in marinara sauce, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 10–12 minutes so flavors meld.
  3. Boil Banza and regular penne in salted water according to box directions (about 7–9 mins). Drain.
  4. Sauté spiralized zoodles in a pan with light olive oil spray for 2 minutes. Blend with Mom's Banza penne.
🥗 Mom's Portion
4 oz 90/10 lean beef Bolognese sauce + 1.5 cups mixed Banza pasta & zoodles + side garden salad with 1 tbsp light dressing + 1 tsp parmesan.
🎯 38g Protein • 14g Fat • 45g Carbs • 460 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Top with heaping grated parmesan, crushed red pepper flakes, and dried Italian oregano.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Warm penne pasta tossed with beef meat sauce, melted shredded mozzarella, and steamed broccoli florets.

Instant Pot Teriyaki Chicken & Snap Peas

Instant Pot ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 15m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce + 1/4 cup honey
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar + 1 tbsp sesame oil + 3 cloves minced garlic + 1 tsp minced ginger
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water (slurry)
  • 8 oz sugar snap peas & 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • Cooked brown rice (jasmine rice for kids)
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Place chicken breasts into Instant Pot. Whisk soy sauce, honey, vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger; pour over chicken.
  2. Seal lid and cook on High Pressure for 10 minutes, followed by a 5-minute natural release then quick release.
  3. Transfer chicken to a cutting board and shred or slice into chunks. Turn Instant Pot to Sauté mode, stir in cornstarch slurry, and simmer for 2 mins until thickened.
  4. Add snap peas and sliced peppers into hot sauce for 2 minutes to tender-crisp. Return chicken to pot.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz teriyaki chicken breast + 1/2 cup brown rice + 1 cup snap peas & bell peppers + 1 tbsp light glaze.
🎯 38g Protein • 12g Fat • 48g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Top with chili garlic crisp oil, sriracha, sliced scallions, and toasted sesame seeds.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Mild shredded teriyaki chicken over jasmine rice + fresh sliced cucumbers.

Air-Fryer Crispy Panko Tenders & Roasted Potatoes

Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 12m 🔥 Cook: 15m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs chicken breast tenderloins
  • 1 cup Panko breadcrumbs (seasoned with garlic powder, paprika, salt, pepper)
  • 1 egg (beaten) or 2 tbsp Dijon mustard for dredging
  • 1.5 lbs baby medley potatoes, quartered
  • 1 bag fresh garden salad with cherry tomatoes
  • Olive oil cooking spray
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Toss quartered baby potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Air-fry at 400°F for 15 minutes until crisp and tender.
  2. Dip chicken tenderloins in beaten egg (or brush with mustard), then press into seasoned panko breadcrumbs.
  3. Lightly spray tenders with olive oil spray. Air-fry at 390°F for 10–12 minutes until deeply golden and 165°F inside.
  4. Toss garden salad with light vinaigrette.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz panko tenders + 110g roasted baby medley potatoes + colorful garden salad with light vinaigrette.
🎯 36g Protein • 14g Fat • 44g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Spicy buffalo sauce dip & creamy blue cheese or ranch dressing.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Crispy panko tenders with sweet honey mustard + roasted potato bites.

🌱 Mediterranean Crispy Chickpea & Grain Bowls

Air Fryer / Bowl ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 15m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) organic chickpeas, rinsed, drained & patted dry
  • 1 tbsp olive oil + 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, pinch salt
  • 3 cups cooked brown rice or quinoa
  • 2 Persian cucumbers, diced + 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese + 1/2 cup prepared deli Tzatziki
  • Warm whole wheat pita bread (for kids/dad)
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Toss thoroughly dried chickpeas with 1 tbsp olive oil, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and salt.
  2. Air-fry chickpeas at 390°F for 12–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until crunchy and lightly browned.
  3. Warm cooked grain (brown rice or quinoa) and warm pita wedges in a dry pan or toaster.
  4. Assemble bowls: base of grains, heaping roasted chickpeas, diced cucumbers, tomatoes, crumbled feta, and a dollop of tzatziki.
🥗 Mom's Portion
1 cup spiced crispy chickpeas + 1/2 cup quinoa/brown rice + diced cucumbers, tomatoes, 2 tbsp feta & 2 tbsp tzatziki.
🎯 28g Protein • 15g Fat • 50g Carbs • 450 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Pickled banana peppers/jalapeños, extra crumbled feta, and a drizzle of spicy chili garlic oil.
🧒 Kids' Plate

Custom Family Burgers (Lean Beef or Turkey)

Grill / Skillet ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 10m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs 90/10 lean beef or 93/7 ground turkey (formed into 5 patties)
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper
  • 1 bag frozen sweet potato fries

🐟 Grilled Cod with Lemon-Caper Butter & Wild Rice

Outdoor Grill / Skillet ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 10m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs wild Atlantic or Pacific Cod fillets
  • 2 tbsp butter + 2 tbsp capers + 1 lemon (juiced) + 1 tbsp fresh parsley
  • 3 cups cooked wild rice blend (Lundberg)
  • 1 lb fresh asparagus, trimmed
  • Kids alternative: 1 lb chicken breast tenders on skewers
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Preheat grill to medium (375°F) with a lightly oiled grill mat or foil packet for fish.
  2. Season cod with salt, pepper, and olive oil spray. Grill 4–5 mins per side until opaque and flaky.
  3. In a small pan, melt butter with capers, lemon juice, and parsley for 2 mins.
  4. Grill asparagus tossed in olive oil for 5–7 mins until tender-crisp. Spoon lemon-caper butter over cod.
🥗 Mom's Portion
6 oz cod fillet + 2/3 cup cooked wild rice blend + grilled fresh asparagus + 1 tbsp lemon-caper butter.

🥩 Grilled Flank Steak Burrito Bowls with Chimichurri

Outdoor Grill / Skillet ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 12m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs lean flank steak (or top sirloin)
  • 2 bell peppers + 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed & drained
  • 3 cups cooked brown rice (cilantro-lime seasoned)
  • Budget Herb Chimichurri: 1/2 bunch finely chopped parsley, 2 cloves minced garlic, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp salt, pinch red pepper flakes
  • Toppings: Fresh salsa, 1 avocado, pickled jalapeños
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Whisk chimichurri ingredients together in a small bowl; set aside.
  2. Season flank steak with olive oil, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Grill over medium-high heat (400°F) for 5–6 mins per side to medium doneness (135°F–140°F). Rest 5 mins, then slice thinly against the grain.
  3. Grill peppers and onions in a basket for 8 mins until lightly charred. Warm black beans in a saucepan.
  4. Build bowls with cilantro-lime brown rice, black beans, fajita peppers, and sliced flank steak.
🥗 Mom's Portion
4.5 oz grilled lean flank steak + 1/3 cup brown rice + 1/3 cup black beans + grilled peppers/onions + fresh salsa + 1/4 sliced avocado.
🎯 38g Protein • 15g Fat • 43g Carbs • 460 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Drizzle generously with fresh homemade chimichurri and add spicy pickled jalapeños.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Mild flank steak slices over rice, black beans, and mild shredded cheddar cheese.

🌱 Loaded Black Bean & Sweet Potato Enchilada Bake

Oven ⏱️ Prep: 15m 🔥 Cook: 20m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes (peeled and diced small)
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) organic black beans, rinsed & drained
  • 1 cup sweet corn kernels
  • 1 jar (16 oz) green Salsa Verde or red enchilada sauce
  • 8 small corn tortillas (cut into quarters)
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, salt
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Microwave diced sweet potatoes in a covered bowl with 2 tbsp water for 5 mins until tender.
  2. Toss sweet potatoes with black beans, corn, cumin, chili powder, and half the enchilada sauce.
  3. In a 9x13 baking dish, layer tortilla quarters, sweet potato-black bean mixture, and sauce. Top with shredded jack cheese.
  4. Bake at 375°F for 18–20 minutes until bubbling and cheese is melted and lightly browned.
🥗 Mom's Portion
1 generous square enchilada bake (black beans, sweet potato, corn, salsa verde, light jack cheese) + side green salad.

Grilled Chicken Soft Tacos with Guacamole

Outdoor Grill ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 12m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs chicken breast tenders
  • 1 packet taco seasoning + 1 lime (juiced) + 1 tbsp oil
  • Corn tortillas (for Mom) & flour tortillas (for kids/dad)
  • Shredded romaine lettuce, fresh salsa, prepared guacamole
  • Shredded cheddar cheese & sour cream
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Toss chicken tenders in oil, lime juice, and taco seasoning.
  2. Grill over medium-high heat for 4–5 mins per side until internal temp is 165°F. Rest and slice.

🌱 Creamy Tuscan White Bean & Spinach Pasta

Stovetop Skillet ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 15m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) organic Cannellini (white kidney) beans, rinsed & drained
  • 1 box (8 oz) Banza chickpea penne (or regular penne for kids)
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced Italian tomatoes + 1/2 cup tomato marinara
  • 4 cups fresh baby spinach
  • 3 cloves minced garlic + 2 tbsp olive oil + 1/4 cup light cream or milk
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan cheese + pinch red pepper flakes
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Boil penne pasta in salted water for 7–8 mins until al dente. Drain.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat and sauté minced garlic for 1 min.
  3. Add drained cannellini beans, diced tomatoes, marinara, and light cream. Simmer gently for 5 mins, slightly mashing some beans to thicken the sauce.
  4. Stir in baby spinach until wilted (1 min). Toss in cooked pasta and parmesan cheese.
🥗 Mom's Portion
3/4 cup cannellini beans + 1.5 oz Banza pasta + creamy garlic-spinach-tomato sauce + 1 tbsp parmesan.
🎯 30g Protein • 13g Fat • 54g Carbs • 460 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Crushed red pepper flakes, heaping parmesan & fresh cracked black pepper.
🧒 Kids' Plate
Penne pasta in creamy tomato sauce with cannellini beans & melted parmesan.

Air-Fryer BBQ Chicken Tenders & Sweet Corn

Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 12m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5–2 lbs chicken breast tenderloins
  • 1/2 cup BBQ sauce (low-sugar for Mom, sweet for kids)
  • 4 ears sweet corn on the cob
  • 1 bag broccoli florets (steamed)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt & pepper
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Preheat Air Fryer to 390°F. Season chicken tenders with garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Air-fry chicken for 8 minutes, brush generously with BBQ sauce, and air-fry 3 more minutes until caramelized and 165°F.
  3. Boil or grill sweet corn for 6–8 minutes. Steam broccoli in microwave or saucepan for 4 mins.
🥗 Mom's Portion
5 oz BBQ glazed chicken breast tenders + 1 ear sweet corn + 1.5 cups steamed broccoli.
🎯 37g Protein • 12g Fat • 46g Carbs • 440 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Spicy coarse Dijon mustard, cayenne pepper & hot sauce swirl.

Sweet corn on the cob + sweet BBQ chicken tenders + broccoli with butter.

🐟 Air-Fryer Garlic Butter Shrimp & Asparagus

Air Fryer ⏱️ Prep: 10m 🔥 Cook: 8m 👥 Serves: 5
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs raw large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails on)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil / butter + 3 cloves minced garlic + 1 lemon (juiced)
  • 1 lb fresh asparagus, trimmed
  • 3 cups cooked wild rice blend
  • Kids alternative: Mini chicken sliders on Hawaiian rolls
👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Toss shrimp and trimmed asparagus with olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  2. Preheat Air Fryer to 390°F. Cook shrimp and asparagus in basket for 6–8 minutes, shaking halfway, until shrimp are pink and asparagus is tender-crisp.
  3. Serve with warm wild rice blend and lemon wedges.
🥗 Mom's Portion
6 oz raw large shrimp + 2/3 cup cooked wild rice blend + roasted asparagus + 1 tbsp garlic butter/oil.
🎯 38g Protein • 15g Fat • 44g Carbs • 460 kcal
🌶️ Dad's Flavor
Garlic chili butter drizzle with lemon & crushed red pepper flakes.
🧒 Kids' Plate (Alternative)
Air-Fryer Mini Chicken Sliders on sweet Hawaiian rolls.
